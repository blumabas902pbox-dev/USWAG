About the System

USWAG SLP System was created to simplify everyday business for the Samahan ng Masisinop SLP. It helps local cooperatives manage their inventory, automatically compute daily sales, and keep safe, verified records of every transaction. This system bridges technical innovation with grassroots community support. Build with passion, love and care.

My Academic Roots

This web-system was developed as part of the Information Technology program at Partido State University, San Juan Buenvista Street, Goa Camarines Sur. It applies concepts from web development and database management to provide a real-world, easy-to-use digital solution for the progress of barangay.

Empowering the SLP Community

The DSWD Sustainable Livelihood Program (SLP) helps families build better lives through livelihood and small businesses. USWAG supports this mission by giving our local farmers and sellers a simple website to easily manage and sell their harvests and products, helping everyone in the community earn a steady income and grow together.
